## Reviewing a service and connecting a flow

Putting voice AI at a customer touchpoint involves more than reviewing a vendor's feature list. The guidance scenario, the entry point in the product, and the return from the phone app all need attention. I compared vendor PoC scope, costs, and integration requirements for overdue-payment reminders and follow-up calls, and coordinated guidance scenarios with the CX team. The [public archive](/en/#archive-vox) describes vendor review and product integration, not AI model development.

In the product, I connected an entry from the returnable subscription completion screen into contract guidance and the flow back from the phone app. After integration, I tracked usage and costs. Implementing entry and return paths does not show whether customers understood a call or resolved a problem. Coordinating a CX scenario also does not mean that I owned the entire consultation operation.

I treat call counts as usage data. The public record contains no evidence for a consultation completion or issue resolution rate, so I do not add one here.
